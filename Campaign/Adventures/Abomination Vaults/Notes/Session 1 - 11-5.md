Hello and welcome to the town of [[Otari]]! A quaint logging and trading port made up of some 1200 people spanning most of the ancestries found crowded around the Inner Sea. Wethere you are an Otrai native, born and bread, or an outsider from places unknown. You've taken a liking to the town and it's people. 

Otari has always been kind to adventurers, the town itself was founded by them. A stroied group calling itself the Rose Gard were dispatched to this area some 500 years ago to deal with a threat. In the end they succeeded, but they lost one of their number. A rouge named Otari Ilvashtie. They knew the threat they faced was defeated, but not destroyed, so they set up a small outpost and named it after their fallen comrade. 

The outpost has an unusual history that we don't need to get into now, but it has grown into a major source of lumber and a trading post on the way to and from the great city of Absolom just a bit further east along the coast. 

What concerns us today is a group of 5 adventures who have come off their first of hopefully many successful feats of daring. You assisted the [[Tamily Tanderveil]] , the owner of the Otari Fishery, with a little pest problem that turned into a bit more than what you were expected. As word got out about what you all did and found under the fishery, the town has taken to calling your group the Rat Catchers.

Wrin welcomes you in and ushers you past the shelves packed with an enless and varied array of bric-a-brac into a room in the back. The large circular room is painted in vivid and colorful depiction of the night sky. Overhead you see a glass dome with an uninterrupted view of the darkening sky as the sun sets. In the middle stands a large round table bedecked in blue silver and black. Comfy chairs sit around the table and in the middle is a large pot of steeping tea and a delicious array of snacks. 

"Please sit where you like the tea should be almost ready and it looks like the stars will be out soon" As you all take your seats she hands you small delicate cups of tea and offers some small biscuits and sandwiches. 

As the room gets dark with the fading sun, candles flicker to life by themselves. All producing a blue flame that renders the plush room in a pale and eerie light. You see her look straight up and she smiles "Excellent" she says. You follow her gaze up to see the sky is filling with stars. Many more than you're used to seeing.
